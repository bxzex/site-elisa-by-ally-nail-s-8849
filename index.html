Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to make an appointment?

While we do accept walk-ins based on availability, we highly recommend booking an appointment in advance to secure your preferred time and technician, especially for intricate nail art.

What products do you use?

We use only premium, industry-leading brands that prioritize nail health. Our polishes and gels are carefully selected to be long-lasting while minimizing damage to your natural nail bed.

How long does custom nail art take?

The time varies depending on the complexity of the design. Simple art may add 15 minutes to your service, while intricate 3D designs or hand-painted characters can take an additional hour. Please mention your art request when booking.

What is your cancellation policy?

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment. This allows us to offer the time slot to other clients.
